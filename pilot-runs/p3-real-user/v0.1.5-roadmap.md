bridge-handover berechnet drift_factor + prüft gegen Memory-historische Range. Bei Abweichung > 2×Std-Dev: WARN.

**Empirie-Anker (ADR_0031 §3.2):**

- Use-Case ohne Profile: drift-range 0.5-1.5 (p4+p5)

**Self-Test:** T49-T50.

## D.2 PB-002 Anti-Endless-Loop Reflection-Action-Ratio (~2-3h)

**ADR_0031 §4.1 Decision:** Domain-aware Threshold-Kalibrierung:

```yaml
ratio_thresholds:
  plugin-self-dev-with-profile: 15.0   # p3-Empirie 12.5
  use-case-without-profile: 4.0        # p4+p5+p6 0.67-2.00
  use-case-with-profile: 5.0           # Hypothese, Empirie n=0
  default: 4.0
```

Lookup via `state.topic_metadata.domain_hint` (Phase G implementiert).

**Empirie-Anker (ADR_0031 §3):** p3-real-user R/A-ratio 12.5 wäre WARN unter aktuellem 4.0-Default — Domain-aware Threshold verhindert false positive.

**Self-Test:** T51-T52.
